# Writing the carousel

## What tends to work

From scoring thousands of carousels against each creator's usual engagement:
- **A number in the hook** ("7 prompts", "the 3 settings") beats a vague promise.
- **7–12 slides** for lists and tutorials. Fewer for a hot take or a story with one turn.
- **Cheat-sheet and save-for-later carousels** get saved, and saves travel.
- **An ask on the last slide** (save this, send it to someone, comment a word) gets more of that action.
- **Showing beats telling**: a before/after, a real number, a real screenshot.

## Formats

Pick one and say which:
- **List**: N things, one per slide. Hook names the number and the payoff.
- **Tutorial**: steps in order, one step per slide, each starting with a verb.
- **Cheat sheet**: dense but scannable, made to be saved. 1–3 slides of reference.
- **Story**: a setup, a turn, a lesson. Every slide ends on a reason to swipe.
- **Hot take**: a claim people will argue with, the reasons, then the nuance.
- **This vs that**: two options side by side across slides, then the verdict.
- **Case study**: the result first, then how, then what to copy.

## The hook (slide 1)

- At most about 10 words. One idea, one promise.
- Concrete: a number, a name, an outcome, a time frame.
- Write 3 with different angles, e.g. the payoff ("Steal my 5 caption formulas"), the problem ("Your carousels die on slide 2, here's why") and the surprise ("The best carousel I posted took 9 minutes").
- A short line under it is fine if it adds a reason to swipe.

## Every other slide

- One idea per slide. A heading of 2–6 words plus at most 2–3 short lines.
- About 30 words maximum. If it needs more, split the slide.
- Lead with the useful part. No warm-up sentences.
- Plain words. No "unlock", "elevate", "game-changer", "delve", "in today's fast-paced world". No em dashes.
- Numbers as digits.
- Each slide should make the next one feel needed.

## The last slide

One ask, said plainly: "Save this for your next post", "Send this to the friend who needs it", or "Comment GUIDE and I'll send you the template". Add the handle.

## The caption

The hook again in one line, 2–3 short lines of value or context, the ask, then 3–5 hashtags at most.
